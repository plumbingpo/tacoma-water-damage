Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.
  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.

Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • The house was flooded when you walked in, with no telling how many hours it had been going.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
